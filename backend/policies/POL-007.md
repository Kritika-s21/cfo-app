## Rules
- Minimum cash buffer: 8 weeks of operating expenses
- Wire transfers above $50K require dual approval
- FX exposures above $100K must be hedged or CFO-approved
- 13-week cash forecast updated every Monday before 10:00

## Approvals
Wire and FX approvals follow the sign-off limits in [[POL-003]].
